FAYE'S STORY

About The Blue Barn

A Woman-Owned Business in the Catskills

The Blue Barn is a woman-owned antiques and design destination in the Catskills, founded by Faye Storms. What began as an antique store in the 1990s has grown into a collection of dealers, unique finds, and a community art gallery.

Faye’s background is in fashion and design. After graduating high school at 16, she attended the Fashion Institute of Technology, earning degrees in fashion design and fashion illustration. She later worked between Los Angeles and New York City with her husband, photographer Peter Hurd, while developing the beginnings of The Blue Barn.

In the 1990s, Faye moved to the Catskills full-time and opened the antique store. Over the following decade, she expanded the property, adding a two-story building and developing a business that brought antiques and reproductions together in a way that was new to the area.

The Blue Barn Today

Today, The Blue Barn is home to 12 independent dealers across two buildings, offering everything from vintage clothing and mid-century furniture to antiques, art, and unusual collectibles.

The property is also home to Gallery 28, a community art gallery featuring local and regional artists, with new exhibitions and openings nearly every month.
